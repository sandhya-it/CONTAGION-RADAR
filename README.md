# 📡 Contagion Radar

### Predicting Financial Shockwaves Before They Hit India's SME Supply Chain

**Contagion Radar** is an AI-powered financial risk intelligence platform designed to help identify, analyze, and visualize how financial distress can spread across interconnected businesses in India's Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSME) ecosystem.



## 📌 Overview

India's MSMEs play a crucial role in the economy, but delayed payments, cash-flow shortages, and financial dependencies can expose them to significant risks.

When a large buyer delays payment to a supplier, that supplier may struggle to pay its own vendors, employees, and lenders. This can trigger a chain reaction of financial stress across the supply chain.

**Contagion Radar aims to make these hidden financial connections visible.**

The platform focuses on identifying potential financial vulnerabilities, exploring how risks may propagate between connected businesses, and supporting earlier risk awareness through an intuitive financial intelligence dashboard.



## 🚨 Problem Statement

Financial distress can spread beyond the business where it originates.

- **Delayed Payments:** Suppliers may wait weeks or months to receive payments from buyers.
- **Cash-Flow Disruptions:** Unpaid invoices can limit a business's ability to meet operating expenses.
- **Supply-Chain Dependencies:** One company's financial difficulties can affect multiple connected businesses.
- **Limited Risk Visibility:** Individual businesses may not have a clear picture of financial risks elsewhere in their supply chain.
- **Reactive Decision-Making:** Risks may become visible only after financial pressure has already intensified.

There is a need for a platform that helps users understand these interconnected risks before they potentially escalate.



## 💡 Our Solution

Contagion Radar provides a centralized approach to exploring financial distress and its potential propagation across SME supply chains.

The platform is designed around five core objectives:

1. **Detect Risk Signals** — Examine financial indicators that may suggest emerging distress.
2. **Connect Business Relationships** — Represent financial dependencies between buyers, suppliers, and other entities.
3. **Trace Risk Propagation** — Explore how one company's financial shock may affect connected businesses.
4. **Visualize Potential Impact** — Make complex financial relationships easier to interpret.
5. **Support Early Intervention** — Help stakeholders investigate risks and consider appropriate mitigation strategies.

## 🔄 How It Works

**Step 1 — Identify a Financial Shock**

Consider an event such as a delayed invoice payment, a buyer default, or a sudden cash-flow shortage.

**Step 2 — Examine Connected Businesses**

Identify suppliers, buyers, and other entities that may be financially connected to the affected business.

**Step 3 — Analyze Potential Risk Propagation**

Explore how the initial disruption could affect connected businesses and create additional financial pressure.

**Step 4 — Visualize the Risk Network**

Present the relationships and potential downstream consequences in an understandable format.

**Step 5 — Support Risk Mitigation**

Use the resulting insights to investigate possible actions, such as prioritizing collections, reviewing credit exposure, or diversifying business relationships.



## 🏭 Example Use Case

Imagine a small manufacturing company supplying components to a large buyer.

1. The buyer delays an invoice payment.
2. The manufacturer experiences a cash-flow shortage.
3. The manufacturer delays payments to its raw-material suppliers.
4. Those suppliers experience financial pressure of their own.
5. The original payment delay potentially creates a wider supply-chain disruption.

Contagion Radar aims to help users explore this chain of events and identify where financial risks could spread.
